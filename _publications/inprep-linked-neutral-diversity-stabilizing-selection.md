Stabilizing selection on a quantitative trait leaves a footprint at linked neutral sites, but the standard tools for linked selection are built around directional selection and sweeps.

This project develops a two-locus moments model, using moment equations and allele frequency spectra, to describe how stabilizing selection shapes linked neutral diversity.
