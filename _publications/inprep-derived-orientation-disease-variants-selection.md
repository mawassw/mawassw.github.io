title: "Information about the derived orientation of disease risk-increasing variants provides power to detect signals of directional selection on diseases"

status: 'In preparation'
excerpt: 'Knowing which allele is derived, not merely which raises risk, adds statistical power to tests for directional selection on disease traits.'
---

Tests for directional selection on disease-associated variation typically use effect sizes and allele frequencies. This project shows that polarizing risk-increasing alleles by their derived or ancestral state carries additional information, and builds that orientation into a more powerful test for directional selection on disease phenotypes.
