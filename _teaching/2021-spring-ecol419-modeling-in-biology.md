Taught upper-level undergraduate and graduate students mathematical modeling, computational simulation methods, and how to read and interrogate scientific literature, working in R, Python, and Mathematica. *Spring 2021.*

The course serves students arriving with very uneven quantitative preparation, so much of the teaching was about building a working intuition for what a model is for — what it abstracts away, and when that abstraction stops being useful — before turning to implementation.
