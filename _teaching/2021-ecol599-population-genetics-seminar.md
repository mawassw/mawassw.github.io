Co-designed the syllabus and led weekly discussion-based seminars on foundational and contemporary population genetics literature, spanning the theoretical and the empirical. *2021&ndash;2023.*

The course was built around reading primary literature closely rather than working from a textbook. Students were responsible for leading discussion of individual papers, which put the emphasis on interrogating assumptions, following an argument through its mathematics, and identifying what a given study could and could not establish.
